# Trademark policy

The code of graphersal-server is licensed under Apache-2.0. The licence covers the code, not the
project's name and logo (Apache-2.0, section 6). graphersal-server follows
[Graphersal's trademark policy](https://github.com/graphersal/graphersal/blob/main/TRADEMARKS.md),
which says how the name "Graphersal" may be used; it applies to "graphersal-server" in the same
way. In short:

You may, without asking:
- refer to Graphersal or graphersal-server by name to describe that your software uses, supports
  or is compatible with it ("runs on graphersal-server", "a client for graphersal-server");
- use the names in articles, talks, tutorials and comparisons;
- distribute unmodified official releases under the name.

Please ask first before:
- naming a fork, a modified build, a product, a hosted service or a company "Graphersal",
  "graphersal-server" or something confusingly similar;
- using the name or logo in a way that suggests your product is official, endorsed by or
  affiliated with the project.

A fork is welcome under its own name; its documentation may say that it is based on
graphersal-server.
